# Top Creative Ideation & Brainstorming Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Digital Whiteboards, Mind Mapping & Real-Time Collaborative Workshops*  
**Last updated: March 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Creative Ideation & Brainstorming**. These tools help teams capture ideas visually, run structured workshops, map thoughts into organized structures, and collaborate in real time—covering infinite-canvas whiteboards, mind-mapping software, and facilitation-focused platforms.

**Examples** include Miro, Mural, FigJam, Lucidspark, Stormboard, MindMeister, Conceptboard, Milanote, Whimsical, and AFFiNE (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: This section highlights every major active project for self-hosted whiteboards, open-source mind mapping, and community-driven ideation platforms—ideal for teams seeking data sovereignty and predictable costs over per-seat SaaS subscriptions.

Contributions welcome! Open an PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

| Product | Description | Pricing (Starting) | Free Tier / Trial Limits |
|---------|-------------|-------------------|--------------------------|
| **[Miro](https://miro.com/)** | Industry-standard infinite-canvas whiteboard with templates, diagramming, and facilitation tools. Positioned for cross-functional workshops and visual planning. | Starter: ~$8/member/month (annual); Business: ~$20/member/month (annual) . | **Free**: 3 editable boards, 10 AI credits/month . Low-resolution exports on free tier . |
| **[Mural](https://www.mural.co/)** | Workshop-first whiteboard designed for facilitated sessions. Lets facilitators guide participants through structured exercises. Free plan allows unlimited members on murals. | Team+: $9.99/member/month (annual); Business: $17.99/member/month (annual, adds SSO) . | **Free**: 3 editable murals at a time, unlimited members . |
| **[FigJam](https://www.figma.com/figjam/)** | Online whiteboard from Figma. Best for design teams already in the Figma ecosystem. Imports Miro boards from vector PDF exports. | Professional (Collab seat): $3/seat/month . | **Free (Starter)**: unlimited drafts . |
| **[Lucidspark](https://lucid.co/)** | Visual collaboration whiteboard within the Lucid suite. Bundling with Lucidchart saves 25%. AI generate/summarize/sort available on all plans (free for now). | Individual: $9/month; Team: $10/user/month (annual) . | **Free**: 3 editable boards . |
| **[Stormboard](https://stormboard.com/)** | Structured ideation platform built around sticky notes, votes, and tasks. Output is a ranked list rather than a drawing. | Personal: Free; Business: per-member pricing (no public USD rate) . | **Free (Personal)**: 5 members per Storm, no guests, no credit card required . No raw CSV/JSON exports on free tier . |
| **[MindMeister](https://www.mindmeister.com/)** | Cloud-based mind mapping tool for organizing ideas and structuring thoughts. Offers free plans with limits alongside paid tiers. | Paid plans available; free tier offered . | **Free plan** available with limits (specific caps not detailed in search results) . |
| **[Conceptboard](https://conceptboard.com/)** | Visual collaboration whiteboard with temporary guest access for externals on the free plan—a feature Miro's free tier does not offer. | Starter: €5/user/month (listed in euros) . | **Free**: 3 active boards, 100 objects each, 250 MB storage, temporary guest access . |
| **[Milanote](https://milanote.com/)** | Visual workspace for creative projects and mood boards. Positioned for organizing ideas, references, and creative assets. | Pro: $9.99/month (annual) or $12.50/month (monthly); Teams: $49/month (up to 10 people, annual) . | **Free**: 100 notes, images, or links . |
| **[Whimsical](https://whimsical.com/)** | Flowchart, wireframe, and mind map tool. Free plan caps objects per month rather than boards, so light use of the same board costs nothing. | Pro: $10/editor/month (annual); Teams available . | **Free**: unlimited boards, 50 board objects and 50 doc blocks per month, watermarked exports, 7-day version history . |
| **[AFFiNE](https://affine.pro/)** | Open-source (MIT for Community Edition) all-in-one workspace merging docs and whiteboard on an edgeless canvas. Positioned as a Notion + Miro alternative. Self-hostable via Docker. | Community Edition: free self-host; paid cloud tiers available . | **Community Edition**: free for self-hosting under MIT license . |

Static deployment with no database or server-side state. A self-hosted Excalidraw or draw.io alternative . |

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **AFFiNE** for docs-and-canvas hyper-fusion with self-hosting, **Excalidraw** (or the self-hosted fork) for hand-drawn diagramming with end-to-end encryption, **WBO** for lightweight real-time brainstorming, and **WiseMapping** for structured mind mapping—all deployable via Docker without per-seat SaaS fees.

## Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.
- Ideation whiteboards often contain sensitive strategic information; enforce access controls and consider self-hosting for confidential brainstorming sessions.
- **Free tier limits vary significantly and change frequently.** Miro, Mural, Lucidspark, and Conceptboard all cap free users at **3 editable boards**. Whimsical instead caps **50 objects per month** with unlimited boards. Stormboard limits free Storms to **5 members** with no guests. FigJam offers **unlimited drafts** on its Starter plan. Whimsical free exports carry watermarks; Conceptboard allows temporary guest access while Miro's free tier does not . Always verify current limits against vendor pages before committing.
